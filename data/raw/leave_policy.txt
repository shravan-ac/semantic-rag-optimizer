
COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave
every calendar year.

Leave Application:
Employees must submit leave requests at least five
working days in advance.

Approval:
All leave requests must be approved by the employee's
reporting manager.

Carry Forward:
Employees can carry forward a maximum of five unused
annual leave days to the next calendar year.

Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave
per year. Medical documentation may be requested for
absences longer than three consecutive working days.

Emergency Leave:
Employees may request emergency leave in unexpected
personal situations. Approval depends on the circumstances.
